# HAXYS CAD on your phone

[← Back](../../README.en.md) · [🇧🇷 Português](../pt/celular.md)

<img src="../imagens/celular.png" alt="HAXYS CAD on a phone" width="320" align="right">

Your phone opens **the same HAXYS CAD running on your PC**, with a touch layout. The AI is the main screen: tap 🎤, say the part and watch it appear.

## What you need

- The PC on, with **HAXYS CAD open**.
- The phone on the **same Wi-Fi** as the PC.
- Android (Chrome) or iPhone (Safari).

## Connect (once)

1. On the PC, click the **📱** button on the top bar. A QR code shows up.
2. On the phone, open the **camera**, point it at the QR code and tap the link.
3. The first time, the phone warns that the connection **"is not private"**. That's normal: the certificate belongs to your PC, not the internet. Tap **Advanced → Proceed** (iPhone: **Show details → visit this website**). This is what enables the microphone.
4. If Windows asks, allow HAXYS CAD on **Private networks**.
5. To get an icon: browser menu → **Add to Home screen** (iPhone: **Share → Add to Home Screen**).

## Using it

- **🎤** talks to the AI. **▾** minimizes the box to see the part; **▴** brings it back.
- **Photos:** attach photos of the part you want to copy.
- **Esc** and **＋ Somar** (add to selection) on the touch bar; long-press = right-click.
- Exporting downloads the file to the phone and also saves it on the PC.
