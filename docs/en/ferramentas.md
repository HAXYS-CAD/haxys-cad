# Tools

[← Back](../../README.en.md) · [🇧🇷 Português](../pt/ferramentas.md)

Tools live in the same tabs and panels as Autodesk Fusion. This is a summary of what HAXYS CAD does today.

## Sketch (2D)

Line, rectangle, circle, arc, polygon, slot, ellipse, spline, point and text. Constraints (parallel, perpendicular, tangent, equal, fix...) and dimensions. Project edges and import DXF.

## Solid: create

Extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, rib, web, pipe, emboss, hole (simple and detailed), thread, fasteners, coil and ready-made shapes: box, cylinder, sphere and torus.

## Solid: modify

Fillet, chamfer, shell, draft, press/pull, offset face, scale, combine (join, cut, intersect), split body, move/copy, mirror, rectangular, circular and path patterns, appearance and material.

## Surface

Thicken, patch holes, offset and create surface bodies from profiles.

## Mesh (STL, OBJ, 3MF)

Insert mesh, planar mesh section, fit curves to the section and turn it into a solid to keep modeling.

## Assembly

Components, joints and joint origins, drag with joints, degrees of freedom, component connections and bill of materials.

## Construct, inspect and annotate

Construction planes, coordinate systems, measure, properties (volume and mass), curvature and isocurve analysis, section, annotations and on-model dimensions (PMI).

## Parameters and configurations

Named parameters and expressions (e.g. `width * 2`), part configurations and rules.

## Files

Open STEP, STL, OBJ, 3MF, FBX, USDZ and DXF. Export STEP, STL and 3MF. **Bring from Fusion:** opens the design that's open in Autodesk Fusion (needs Fusion with its official MCP connector on).

## HAXYS (our own)

- 🎤 **Talk to the AI:** builds from voice or text, and replicates parts from photos. See [AI](ia.md).
- 🖨 **Print:** sends to Bambu Studio. See [Printing](impressao.md).
- ✂ **Laser:** creates SVG for laser cutting.
- 🖼 **Part from image** and on-screen **reference image**.
- 📜 **Script console:** build parts with code (`haxys` API), handy for repetitive dimensions.
- 📱 **Phone:** opens HAXYS CAD on your phone over Wi-Fi. See [Phone](celular.md).
- 🎨 **Themes:** seven screen color themes, in the picker next to File.
